Species for EDAR analysis

Primates (reference clade)

- Homo sapiens (human): reference
- Pan troglodytes (chimpanzee): ancestral EDAR allele and clostest living relative
- Carlito syrichta (Philippine tarsier)
- 

Non-Primate mammals

- Rousettus aegyptiacus (Egyptian fruit bat): reduced body hair
- Eumetopias jubatus (Steller sea lion): reduced fur and modified whiskers
- Loxodonta africana (African elephant): near-complete hair loss
- Trichechus manatus (West Indian manatee): vibrissae-dominated integument with near-total hair reduction and aquatic adaptation

Birds

- Acanthisitta chloris (rifleman): 
- Phaethon lepturus (White-tailed tropicbird): early-diverging neoavian lineage
- Anser cygnoides (swan goose): independently domesticated in East Asia
- Opisthocomus hoazin (hoatzin): unique foregut fermentation and chick wing claws
- 

Reptiles:

- Alligator mississippiensis (American alligator): link reptilian and avians
- Python bivittatus (Burmese python): limbless
- Chrysemys picta (painted turtle)
-

Fish:

- Acipenser ruthenus (sterlet)
- Oncorhynchus clarkii lewisi (westslope cutthroat trout): scale variantions
- Cyprinus carpio (common carp): Scale polymorphism in the context of domestication 
- Sinocyclocheilus grahami (golden-line barbel): reduced scales and eyes
- Scyliorhinus canicula (small-spotted catshark): outgroup
- Petromyzon marinus (sea lamprey): scaless
